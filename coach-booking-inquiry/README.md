# Coach & Consultant Booking Inquiry Starter

A dark-mode discovery call inquiry page for coaches, advisors, and solo consultants with zero backend code.

## Setup in 60 seconds

1. Create a free form at [formroost.com](https://formroost.com) and add your site address under **Allow your website**.
2. Replace `fpub_REPLACE_ME` in `index.html` with your FormRoost sandbox ID.
3. Serve locally (`npx serve .`) or deploy to Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, Netlify, or Replit.
4. Test a submission, then publish in FormRoost and switch `/sandbox/` to `/production/`.
