# Small Business Quote Starter

A zero-build-step "Request a free quote" page for local service businesses (plumbers, landscapers, contractors, salons) built with plain HTML, Tailwind, and FormRoost.

## Setup in 60 seconds

1. Create a free form at [formroost.com](https://formroost.com) and add your site or preview URL under **Allow your website**.
2. Replace `fpub_REPLACE_ME` in `index.html` with your FormRoost sandbox ID.
3. Serve the folder (`npx serve .`) or upload it to Cloudflare Pages, Netlify Drop, or GitHub Pages.
4. Test a quote submission, then click **Publish to production** in FormRoost and change `/sandbox/` to `/production/` in `index.html`.
