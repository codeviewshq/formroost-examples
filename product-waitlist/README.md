# Product Waitlist Starter

A lightweight early-access waitlist form with built-in spam protection and CSV export in the FormRoost inbox.

## Setup in 60 seconds

1. Create a free form at [formroost.com](https://formroost.com) and add your site address under **Allow your website**.
2. Replace `fpub_REPLACE_ME` in `index.html` with your FormRoost sandbox ID.
3. Deploy anywhere static sites run, send a test signup, then publish and switch `/sandbox/` to `/production/`.
