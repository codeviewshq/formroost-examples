# Creative & Photography Portfolio Inquiry Starter

A booking inquiry page for photographers, designers, and creative studios—keeps your custom layout with zero backend setup.

## Setup in 60 seconds

1. Create a free form at [formroost.com](https://formroost.com) and add your site address under **Allow your website**.
2. Replace `fpub_REPLACE_ME` in `index.html` with your FormRoost sandbox ID.
3. Deploy anywhere static sites run, send a test inquiry, then publish and switch `/sandbox/` to `/production/`.
